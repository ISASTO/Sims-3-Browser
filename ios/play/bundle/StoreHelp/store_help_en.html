Q: How am I charged for something I purchase within the game (In-App Commerce / microtransaction)?
A: You are billed/charged the same way for any other purchase on your device; through your iTunes account. iTunes is a trademark of Apple Inc., registered in the U.S. and other countries. If you have any billing disputes or questions, please contact Apple. Apple is a trademarks of Apple Inc., registered in the U.S. and other countries

Q: I purchased something in-game, but I cannot see it. What happened?
A: Exit to the Main menu and re-enter to the Sims Store. Check the status of purchased item. If the item is not available, select the 'Restore Purchases' button in-game. The purchased item should become available.

Q: I purchased something on my iPhone. Can I get this same content on my iPod/ iPad (or vice-versa)? iPhone, iPod, and iPad are trademarks of Apple Inc., registered in the U.S. and other countries.
A: Yes, as long as those devices share the same iTunes account. First, start the game on the device you want the new content on (if you don't have the game on that device yet, enter the iTunes store and download it). Then, enter the Sims Store and select 'Restore Purchases' button in-game. All previously purchased items should become available.

Q: My question is not answered here. What can I do?
A: You can visit the FAQ sections at https://support.apple.com/itunes and contact Apple for any billing issues, or visit https://help.ea.com and write us. When contacting us, please include as much specific information as possible so that we can better assist you.
